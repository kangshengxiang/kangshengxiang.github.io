Breast Cancer · TCM Supportive Care Research

TCM supportive care research in breast cancer

Across treatment and recovery, we study the potential role of TCM in symptom management, functional recovery, quality of life, and longer-term wellbeing, with clear attention to safety and evidence limits.

Research Positioning

Support throughout treatment and recovery

Research addresses supportive care needs while appropriate standard treatment remains central.

Breast cancer care may involve surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, endocrine therapy, targeted therapy, or immunotherapy. Studies should record disease stage, molecular subtype, treatment phase, and concurrent interventions so that different populations are not combined without justification.

The aim is to describe and evaluate effects on defined symptoms, recovery, and quality of life—not to present supportive research as evidence for replacing anticancer treatment or curing cancer.

Research Areas

Principal areas of study

Treatment-related symptoms, recovery, and longer-term health are translated into clearly defined and measurable questions.

01

Treatment-related symptoms

Fatigue, sleep disturbance, hot flushes, pain, digestive symptoms, and their effects on daily life.

02

Perioperative recovery

Safe supportive approaches addressing physical capacity, upper-limb function, mobility, and recovery experience.

03

Endocrine therapy support

Hot flushes, joint symptoms, sleep, and adherence-related outcomes without interfering with prescribed therapy.

04

Emotional wellbeing and quality of life

Anxiety, mood, social function, body image, and overall quality of life through patient-reported outcomes.

05

Safety and concurrent medicines

Adverse events and laboratory measures, with attention to risks when herbs are used with endocrine or targeted medicines.

06

Long-term follow-up

Persistent symptoms, health behaviours, return to work, social participation, and sustainable rehabilitation support.

Methods & Outcomes

Methods, outcomes, and integrity

Research design begins with a clearly defined population, treatment context, and set of prespecified outcomes.

Study framework

Participants are described by stage, subtype, and treatment phase, with appropriate comparison, adherence recording, and follow-up.

Core outcomes

Symptoms, quality of life, patient-reported outcomes, functional status, treatment completion, safety, and long-term follow-up.

Ethics and quality

Ethics review, informed consent, privacy, data quality, and transparent reporting, including negative and inconclusive findings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does TCM research replace standard breast cancer treatment?

No. The programme studies supportive care and rehabilitation alongside appropriate treatment. It does not replace surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, endocrine therapy, targeted therapy, or other treatment recommended by qualified teams.

Which symptoms may be studied?

Depending on the protocol, research may address fatigue, sleep, hot flushes, pain, emotional wellbeing, digestive symptoms, and functional recovery, while recording safety and concurrent treatment.

Can website information be used to make medication decisions?

No. The website presents research information and does not provide individual prescriptions or replace professional assessment and medication guidance.

Medical notice

This page describes research interests and does not provide medical advice. People with breast cancer require assessment by qualified healthcare teams. TCM use must consider disease stage, molecular subtype, treatment plan, concurrent medicines, and individual risk. Do not alter standard treatment without professional advice.
