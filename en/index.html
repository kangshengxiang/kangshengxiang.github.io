About the Institute

A responsible platform for TCM research

The Institute responds to the need for responsible innovation and modern research in traditional Chinese medicine.

Our interests include foundational TCM theory, integrative research methods, cancer-related symptom management, and rehabilitation support. We begin with meaningful questions, use appropriate methods, and focus on patient-centred and academically valuable outcomes.

  • Research and clinical care are clearly distinguished; website content does not replace individual medical care
  • Ethics, informed consent, data quality, and accurate reporting are central
  • Collaboration across TCM, conventional medicine, pharmacy, and data science is encouraged

Research Focus

Core research areas

Our work connects foundational research capability with focused programmes in cancer supportive care.

01

Foundational TCM research

Research into TCM theory, pattern characteristics, herbal formula mechanisms, and structured interpretation of traditional experience.

02

Integrative research methods

Study designs, outcome measures, and evidence frameworks that respect the characteristics of TCM while enabling rigorous evaluation.

03

Cancer supportive care

Research on treatment-related symptoms, quality of life, nutrition, sleep, and rehabilitation without replacing standard cancer treatment.

04

Liver cancer research

TCM supportive care research addressing symptoms, functional status, safety, and quality of life within comprehensive treatment.

 05

Breast cancer research

TCM research across the treatment pathway, including symptom management, recovery, and patient-reported outcomes.
06

Research integrity and communication

Clear protocols, sound data management, accurate reporting, and public communication that does not overstate evidence.

Collaboration

Ways to collaborate

We welcome responsible and transparent partnerships in study design, academic exchange, real-world observation, and research communication.

Joint study design

Developing research questions, protocols, outcomes, quality controls, and statistical approaches.

Academic exchange and training

Programmes in TCM research methods, evidence-based medicine, and responsible scientific communication.

Research communication

Presenting methods and findings accurately, with clear evidence limitations and disclosure of relevant interests.

Medical and research notice

This website presents research interests and academic information. It does not provide diagnosis, prescriptions, or personalised medical advice, and it does not claim that any research approach cures cancer. Patients should receive appropriate assessment and standard treatment from qualified healthcare professionals. Any TCM intervention requires individual professional evaluation.
