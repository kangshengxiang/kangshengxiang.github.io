Liver Cancer · TCM Supportive Care Research

TCM supportive care research in liver cancer

Within standard treatment and multidisciplinary care, we study the potential supportive role of TCM in symptoms, functional status, quality of life, recovery, and safety.

Research Positioning

Supportive care, not a replacement for treatment

Research is conducted in the context of appropriate surgery, ablation, interventional therapy, radiotherapy, and systemic treatment.

People with liver cancer differ in disease status, liver function, treatment stage, and comorbidities. Studies should therefore define participants and treatment context clearly while examining symptoms, physical function, sleep, appetite, quality of life, adverse events, and possible medicine interactions.

Any conclusion must reflect the study design, sample size, comparator, and follow-up period. Early observations and individual cases should never be presented as established efficacy.

Research Areas

Principal areas of study

Research questions are designed to be clinically relevant, measurable, and meaningful to patients.

01

Patterns and symptoms

Relationships between TCM patterns and fatigue, appetite, sleep, pain, and other symptoms across treatment stages.

02

Support during treatment

Effects on symptom burden and treatment tolerance without delaying or replacing standard care.

03

Quality of life and function

Validated measures of daily activity, emotional wellbeing, sleep, social function, and overall quality of life.

04

Safety and interactions

Adverse events, laboratory safety measures, and potential interactions between herbal and anticancer medicines.

05

Real-world observation

Responsible collection of routine care and outcome data to describe practice and inform future confirmatory research.

06

Follow-up and rehabilitation

Persistent symptoms, nutrition, physical recovery, and practical frameworks for longer-term follow-up.

Methods & Outcomes

Methods, outcomes, and integrity

Prospective observation, controlled studies, real-world research, or systematic review may be selected according to the question.

Study framework

Clear participants, intervention, comparator, and outcomes; defined follow-up; complete recording of concurrent treatment; appropriate bias control and analysis.

Core outcomes

Symptom scales, patient-reported outcomes, ECOG or other functional measures, quality of life, safety, and treatment completion.

Ethics and quality

Appropriate ethics review and consent, privacy protection, data dictionaries, quality control, and transparent reporting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does TCM research replace standard liver cancer treatment?

No. This programme studies supportive care and related outcomes alongside appropriate comprehensive treatment. Patients should follow treatment plans developed by qualified healthcare teams.

Which outcomes may be studied?

Outcomes may include treatment-related symptoms, quality of life, physical and functional status, nutrition, sleep, safety, and patient-reported outcomes defined in advance by each study protocol.

Does this website provide personalised prescriptions?

No. The website presents research information and cannot replace in-person medical assessment, diagnosis, or prescribing.

Medical notice

This page describes research interests and does not provide medical advice. People with liver cancer require assessment by qualified multidisciplinary teams. TCM use must consider liver function, previous treatment, concurrent medicines, and individual risk. Do not stop or alter standard treatment without professional advice.
